# Vendor Management Policy (Sample)

*Fictional sample policy for the AuditLens demo. Not any real company's policy.*

## 2. Requirements

### 2.1 Vendor approval
Any new third-party service that receives company or customer data — including SaaS APIs, AI model providers and hosted vector databases — must complete a security review and be added to the approved vendor list before production use.

### 2.2 Contracts
Vendors processing Confidential data must sign a DPA. AI providers must contractually commit not to train on company data.

### 2.3 Monitoring
Critical vendors are reassessed annually.
